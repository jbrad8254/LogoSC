# LogoSC Nuts & Bolts — Customizable Printable Fasteners

Create bolts, screws, nuts, assemblies, and selected thread profiles with OpenSCAD's Customizer.
Choose metric or custom dimensions, thread form, head, drive, length, fit, and resolution. Included
galleries cover hex, pan, round, carriage, countersunk, and headless styles plus V, Whitworth,
square, trapezoidal, Acme, and buttress profiles.

The ZIP contains the self-contained model, detailed calibration and algorithm guide, deterministic
tests, illustrated suite guide, and source-version record.

Printed fasteners are not structurally rated. Material, printer, slicer, orientation, wear, fit,
and loading all matter; independently validate safety-relevant uses.

Source and bug reports:
<https://github.com/jbrad8254/LogoSC>

LogoSC was developed through sustained human-directed collaboration with OpenAI AI coding
assistants; project direction, verification, and releases remain under human control.
